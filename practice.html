Progetto Fulgido is an artistic practice that explores instinctive drawing and through its practice it unlocks the authentic gesture that emerges when the mind stops controlling and the body begins to speak.

​

Born from the encounter between Japanese ink, smooth paper and organic shapes, it is a space for cultivating the here and now: the contemplation of a warm sun, the sinuousness of an Atlantic seaweed, the silence of a mark that glides.

Valeria is an italian artist based in milano. Using black ink, calligraphy brushes and smooth white paper her practice explores intuition, thought, gestures and transformative observation.
